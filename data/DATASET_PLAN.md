Project: Detecting Stance in Media on Global Warming for Indic Languages

Claim:
"Climate change and global warming is a serious concern."

Languages:
1. Hindi
2. Bengali

Labels:
1. Favour
2. Against
3. None

Favour:
The comment supports/agrees that climate change or global warming
is a serious concern.

Against:
The comment disagrees with or rejects the claim.

None:
The comment is neutral, unrelated, or does not clearly express
a stance toward the claim.

Target domain:
YouTube comments
